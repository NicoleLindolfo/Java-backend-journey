## 📌 About this repository

This is the public record of my 12-month journey to become a **Junior Backend Software Engineer in Java**, focused on high-criticality, high-scale systems (fintech, payments, data infrastructure).

Here you'll find:
- The **full roadmap** I'm following ([`ROADMAP.md`](./ROADMAP.md))
- My **tracked progress** at 2, 6, 8, and 12-month checkpoints ([`PROGRESS.md`](./PROGRESS.md))
- My parallel **technical English** track (zero → B1) ([`english-track.md`](./english-track.md))
- Code, exercises, and projects organized by study phase

Every new study session results in a commit — the commit history itself is meant to be an honest, verifiable record of my progress.

## 🎯 Goal

Land my first position as a **Junior Backend Engineer in Java**, working on systems that demand correctness, reliability, and scale — not generic backend — reaching B1 English and building a portfolio that demonstrates real engineering competence, not just course completion.

12 months is the **baseline target**, not a hard deadline. The roadmap is organized by phase, not by calendar date — if a phase takes longer than expected, that's fine; the plan simply continues. Progress is tracked by what I can actually do, not by whether a date was hit.

## 🧭 Principles guiding this study

- **Fundamentals before tools.** I don't jump to frameworks or advanced tooling without understanding what's underneath.
- **Mastery is measured by capability, not hours.** The bar is: can I solve, explain, debug, and justify decisions — alone, without a tutorial?
- **Every technical decision is validated against the real market**, not intuition or trends.
- **Portfolio projects demonstrate engineering, not decoration** — documentation, tests, and explained architectural decisions.
