# 🇺🇸 Technical English — Zero → B1 in 12 Months

English is part of the career, not a separate activity — documentation, interviews, and professional communication in the international market all require it.

## Goal

Go from **zero** to **B1 (CEFR)** in 12 months, focused on technical English (reading documentation, writing READMEs/commits, interview communication).

## Required pace

CEFR estimates suggest around 350–400 study hours to go from zero to B1 — roughly **1 hour per day** of consistent practice.

## How this shows up in this repository

- **This entire repository is written in English from day one** — README, roadmap, commit messages, code comments. That's the practice: not a separate exercise, but the default language of the work itself.
- Early on, sentences may be simple or imperfect — that's expected and fine. The commit history itself becomes a visible record of improvement over time.
